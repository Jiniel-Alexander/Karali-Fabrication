## 1. Strict Evidence-Only Messaging Rule (Zero Fabrication)

**NEVER invent, assume, or extrapolate:**
- Company physical address, workshop square footage, or pin code (unless explicitly provided by the owner)
- Years in business, project counts (e.g., *"500+ projects delivered"*), or tonnage fabricated
- ISO certifications, BIS stamps, AWS/ASME welder certifications, or safety ratings
- Client names, contractor logos, case studies, or customer testimonials
- Guaranteed delivery timelines or pricing figures

**When a claim would strengthen a section but evidence is not yet provided:**
- Use an explicit, clearly marked owner-input placeholder in documentation or ask the owner for the exact verified fact rather than inventing it.
- Focus copy on **concrete fabrication capabilities, clear scope of work, service areas (Mumbai, Thane, Navi Mumbai / MMR), and fast direct communication (`+91 79775 91792` Call & WhatsApp)**.

## 2. Industrial Copywriting Principles (`copywriting`)

1. **Clarity Over Cleverness:**
   - Contractors, architects, structural engineers, society committees, and industrial buyers scan for **exact scope**:
     - *Structural steel fabrication & erection*
     - *Lift shaft steelwork, crash decks & working platforms*
     - *Industrial sheds, trusses & mezzanine floors*
     - *Custom metal fabrication, staircases, railings & gates*
2. **Ban AI Copy Tells:**
   - Never use *"It's not X, it's Y"* reveals, *"No X, no Y, no Z"* triplets, *"Unleash"*, *"Elevate"*, *"Seamless"*, *"Cutting-edge"*, or em-dash-heavy run-on sentences.
   - Use direct, active verbs: *fabricate, weld, assemble, install, cut, erect, reinforce*.
3. **Action-Oriented CTAs:**
   - Pair every primary section with a specific, low-friction CTA:
     - Urgent / Site-ready buyers: **Call `+91 79775 91792`** (`tel:+917977591792`)
     - Drawing / BoQ / Photo sharers: **WhatsApp Us** (`https://wa.me/917977591792`)
     - Structured enquiries: **Request a Quote** / **Submit Locality**

## 3. Page & Form Conversion Optimization (`cro`)

1. **Above-the-Fold Hierarchy:**
   - Within 3 seconds on mobile or desktop, a visitor must see:
     1. **Who & What:** Karali Fabrication — Metal Fabrication & Structural Steel
     2. **Where:** Serving Mumbai, Thane, and Navi Mumbai (MMR)
     3. **Immediate Next Step:** One-tap Call, WhatsApp, or Quote/Locality form
2. **Form Friction Reduction:**
   - Ask only for fields required to route and respond to the enquiry.
   - Use searchable, keyboard- and touch-friendly selectors (like the 57-locality combobox) instead of long unsearchable dropdowns.
   - Show clear privacy reassurance directly below the submit button explaining exactly what is stored and why.
   - Never dead-end a user on a form error: always include direct Call and WhatsApp fallback options in error/unavailable messages.
