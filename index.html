How We Execute

From Structural Drawing to On-Site Erection

Sectors We Serve

  • Civil & High-Rise Cores Lift shaft steelwork, divider beams, crash decks, working platforms, and primary structural steel framing.
  • MIDC Factories & Warehouses Industrial sheds, mezzanine floors, machine skids, pipe supports, safety handrails, and loading canopies.

3-Step Project Workflow

  1. Step 01
    Drawing, BoQ or Site Review

    Send your structural drawings, Bill of Quantities (BoQ), or site dimensions via WhatsApp or phone.

  2. Step 02
    Scope Alignment & Itemised Quote

    We align on section profiles, connection details, surface finish, and site access to issue a clear quotation.

  3. Step 03
    Fabrication, Welding & Erection

    Shop cutting, drilling, and welding followed by coordinated delivery and on-site erection across MMR.
