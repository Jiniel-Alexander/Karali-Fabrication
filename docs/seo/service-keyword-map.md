### Four Core Intent Types Addressed:
1. **B2B Procurement Intent (High Value):** Engineers, architects, and civil contractors searching for drawing-based fabrication (`"structural steel fabricators in mumbai"`, `"elevator crash deck manufacturer"`).
2. **Facility / Plant Expansion Intent (High Value):** Factory owners and warehouse managers needing space expansion (`"industrial mezzanine floor fabricator thane"`, `"factory shed construction navi mumbai"`).
3. **Commercial & Society Intent (Mid Value):** Housing society secretaries and commercial builders needing perimeter or access infrastructure (`"fire escape staircase fabricator"`, `"motorized compound gate fabricator mumbai"`).
4. **Custom Jobbing Intent (Mid Value):** Contractors needing specialized base plates, skids, or on-site welding (`"custom metal fabrication mumbai"`, `"on site welding contractor thane"`).

## 3. Local MMR Geo-Targeting Guidelines (Anti-Doorway Architecture)

To maximize local search visibility without triggering Google's **Helpful Content / Doorway Page penalty**:
* **DO NOT** create 57 nearly identical pages like `/services/structural-steel-fabrication/andheri/`, `/services/structural-steel-fabrication/powai/`. This is programmatic doorway spam and causes algorithmic de-indexing.
* **DO** maintain the client-side Locality Combobox on the homepage and quote form, which provides instant user utility across all 57 localities without creating thin HTML pages.
* **DO** reference genuine industrial clusters organically within parent service pages (e.g. *"Executing structural steelwork across industrial estates including Rabale, Pawane, Taloja, Dombivli, Wagle Estate, and Andheri East"*).
* **DO** create at most **3 regional industrial hub pages** in Phase 4 if substantial unique project evidence exists:
  1. `/locations/navi-mumbai-industrial/` (Focus: Rabale, Pawane, Mahape, Taloja MIDC warehouses & plants)
  2. `/locations/thane-industrial/` (Focus: Wagle Estate, Kolshet, Ghodbunder, Bhiwandi logistics)
  3. `/locations/mumbai-commercial/` (Focus: Kurla, Kanjurmarg, Andheri, high-rise lift shafts & retrofits)
